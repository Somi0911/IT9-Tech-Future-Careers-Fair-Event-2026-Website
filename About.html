Welcome to the Tech Future Careers Fair Event

All about the event

This event is designed for students and job seekers interested in technology careers. It offers a unique opportunity to meet industry professionals, explore real job roles, and discover the pathways that lead to successful careers in tech. The fair is designed to boost confidence, inspire future ambitions, and help you understand exactly what employers are looking for.

  • Software Development
     Learn what it takes to build apps, websites, and digital tools used worldwide.
  • Cybersecurity
     Discover how security experts protect companies from online threats.
  • Data & AI
     Explore careers in analysing data, building AI models, and solving complex problems.
  • IT Support & Networking
     Understand the roles that keep computers, networks, and systems running smoothly.
  • Digital Marketing & Content Creation
     See how tech helps businesses grow through social media and online tools.
  • UX/UI Design
     Learn how designers create engaging, user‑friendly digital experiences.
  • Cloud Computing
     Explore how cloud engineers manage systems used by companies around the world.
